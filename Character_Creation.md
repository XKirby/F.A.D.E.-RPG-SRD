# Character Creation
*__Character Creation__* is the process that both players and gamemasters follow before placing characters of importance within their world. There are several properties and values to calculate and build during this process to fully realize any particular character. Some of these properties are mentioned in both the *Core Rules* and *Introduction* pages and will be further explained and fleshed out here.

## Base Stats
Each character has *__Base Stats__* of 5; In other words, **Physique**, **Ego**, and **Instinct** all start at a minimum value of 5. With this in mind, their **Health**, **Stamina**, and **Energy** start at maximums of 10.

## Major Properties
Besides the previously mentioned, each character has various *__Major Properties__* that they start with. These properties are as followed:
- *__Level:__* A character's **Level** is a quick determining factor for how strong a character is. Whenever you gain one or more **Levels**, you become more powerful. Characters start at **Level** 0. The GM chooses what level the players can start and end at.
- *__Size:__* A character's **Size** determines how large or how small that character is; A larger character has more **Health** at the cost of **Evasion**, whereas a smaller character has more **Evasion** as the cost of their **Health**. The following **Sizes** are available:
  - *Tiny:* A *Tiny* character starts with 80 **Evasion** and has one fifth the **Health** of a *Medium* character. *Tiny* characters are less than or equal to 1 foot tall.
  - *Small:* A *Small* character starts with 60 **Evasion** and has one half the **Health** of a *Medium* character. *Small* characters are close to about 2 to 3 feet tall.
  - *Medium:* A *Medium* character has normal **Evasion** and **Health**. *Medium* characters range from 4 to 7 feet tall.
  - *Large:* A *Large* character starts with 40 **Evasion** and has one and a half times as much **Health** as a *Medium* character. *Large* characters range from 8 to 10 feet tall.
  - *Huge:* A *Huge* character starts with 30 **Evasion** and has two times as much **Health** as a *Medium* character. *Huge* characters range from 11 to 20 feet tall.
  - *Gargantuan:* A *Gargantuan* character starts with 20 **Evasion** and has three times as much **Health** as a *Medium* character. *Gargantuan* characters range from 25 to 40 feet tall. Players can't start as *Gargantuan* characters.
  - *Titanic:* A *Titanic* character starts with 0 **Evasion** and has five times as much **Health** as a *Medium* character. *Titanic* characters range from 50 to 200 feet tall. Players can't start as *Titanic* characters.
- *__Defense:__* As mentioned on other pages, **Defense** reduces incoming damage and characters start with 0 **Global Defense**.
- *__Evasion:__* Also mentioned on other pages, **Evasion** lets you attempt to outright evade effects that happen to target you and characters start with 50 **Evasion**.
