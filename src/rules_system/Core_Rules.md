# Core Rules
This page lists the *__Core Rules__* the system uses, which are based on the **Core Concepts** found in the *Introduction* page.

## Base Stats
The *__Base Stats__* each character has within this system are **<a style="color:#ff4040">Physique</a>**, **<a style="color:#40ff40">Ego</a>**, and **<a style="color:#4040ff">Instinct</a>**. As mentioned within the *Introduction* page, **<a style="color:#ff4040">Physique</a>** represents your physical being, **<a style="color:#40ff40">Ego</a>** represents your mental knowledge, and **<a style="color:#4040ff">Instinct</a>** represents your willpower. Each of the *__Base Stats__* scale up and down in increments of 5. Additionally, each *pair* of them grant a specific resource, found below.

### Health, Stamina, and Energy
*__<a style="color:#ff4040"><i class="fa-solid fa-heart"></i>Health</a>, <a style="color:#40ff40"><i class="fa-solid fa-person-running"></i>Stamina</a>, and <a style="color:#4040ff"><i class="fa-solid fa-bolt"></i>Energy</a>__* are the primary resources each character has in this system. Each resource serves a unique purpose, as listed below:

*__<a style="color:#ff4040"><i class="fa-solid fa-heart"></i>Health</a>__* is equal to **<a style="color:#ff4040">Physique</a>** + **<a style="color:#4040ff">Instinct</a>** and determines the life force of that character.
  - If a character's *__<a style="color:#ff4040"><i class="fa-solid fa-heart"></i>Health</a>__* hits zero, that character is knocked unconscious and can no longer act.
  - If a character's *__<a style="color:#ff4040"><i class="fa-solid fa-heart"></i>Health</a>__* hits into the negative, that character starts dying by bleeding out slowly. Characters that bleed out lose *__<a style="color:#ff4040"><i class="fa-solid fa-heart"></i>Health</a>__* equal to their **Level** at the start of their turn.
  - At the start of that Character's turn after potential bleed out from dying, if you're still unconscious or dying, you may expend 5 *__<a style="color:#4040ff"><i class="fa-solid fa-bolt"></i>Energy</a>__* to perform a **Check** requiring **<a style="color:#4040ff">Instinct</a>** + **d%** with a *Target Value* of 50, plus how much *__<a style="color:#ff4040"><i class="fa-solid fa-heart"></i>Health</a>__* you're in the negative. If you succeed, set your *__<a style="color:#ff4040"><i class="fa-solid fa-heart"></i>Health</a>__* to 1 and you stablize, no longer unconscious or dying.
  - Characters die whenever their *__<a style="color:#ff4040"><i class="fa-solid fa-heart"></i>Health</a>__* hits negatively equal to or lower than its maximum value.

*__<a style="color:#40ff40"><i class="fa-solid fa-person-running"></i>Stamina</a>__* is equal to **<a style="color:#ff4040">Physique</a>** + **<a style="color:#40ff40">Ego</a>** and determines the physical and mental force of that character.
  - Characters can't use actions that require more than their current *__<a style="color:#40ff40"><i class="fa-solid fa-person-running"></i>Stamina</a>__*.
  - Normally, *__<a style="color:#40ff40"><i class="fa-solid fa-person-running"></i>Stamina</a>__* can only be reduced down to a minimum of zero.

*__<a style="color:#4040ff"><i class="fa-solid fa-bolt"></i>Energy</a>__* is equal to **<a style="color:#40ff40">Ego</a>** + **<a style="color:#4040ff">Instinct</a>** and determines the spiritual force of that character.
  - Utilizing your *__<a style="color:#4040ff"><i class="fa-solid fa-bolt"></i>Energy</a>__* as a resource is extremely risky as it directly impacts your soul; Characters can't recover *__<a style="color:#ff4040"><i class="fa-solid fa-heart"></i>Health</a>__* unless they have at least zero *__<a style="color:#4040ff"><i class="fa-solid fa-bolt"></i>Energy</a>__*, and characters that hit *__<a style="color:#4040ff"><i class="fa-solid fa-bolt"></i>Energy</a>__* into the negative equal to or lower than their maximum **snuff out their souls**.

> [!CAUTION]
> Any character that snuffs out their own soul, **including player characters**, <a style="color:red"><u>*__relinquishes their free will__*</u></a>. As a GM, you may decide what this means. Examples include permanent character death, taking full control of that character once they recover, demonic or otherworldly possession, etc. <a style="color:orange"><u>*__YOU MUST MAKE SURE YOUR PLAYERS KNOW WHAT THEY'RE GETTING THEMSELVES INTO IF THEY DO THIS.__*</u></a>

## Aptitudes
*__Aptitudes__* are used to determine how effective you are with certain **Abilities** beyond average capabilities. *__Aptitudes__* should be vague enough to be useful, but precise enough to not encompass everything. *(For example, "Melee Aptitude" could work if your character is generally good with melee abilities, while "Gear Aptitude" is too vague and can encompass far too much of gameplay.)*

An **Aptitude Boost** is equal to +5 in a positive *__Aptitude__*, while an **Aptitude Penalty** is equal to -5 in a negative *__Aptitude__*. Characters can have up to a number of **Boosts** equal to **Level** x 2 + **Penalties** and up to a number of **Penalties** equal to **Level** x 2. **Penalties** are optional; You choose to actively take them to receive **Boosts** in other ways.

## Abilities and Action Points
Characters have a pool of *__<i class="fa-solid fa-gem"></i>Action Points__* that allow them to act a certain number of times within a dangerous situation. Characters are initially created having a maximum of 3 *__<i class="fa-solid fa-gem"></i>Action Points__*.

*__Abilities__* are the general way characters interact within the game and can consume anywhere from 0 to 3 *__<i class="fa-solid fa-gem"></i>Action Points__* as an upfront cost. *__Abilities__* have various **Properties**, such as *Requirements* needed to fully activate, *Prerequisites* to learn them, a *Frequency* with which they can activate in succession, a *Duration* to determine how long they last, and more. These will be further explained in the **Creating Abilities** page.

> [!NOTE]
> *__Abilities__* use their name as a reference to determine where their **Effects** come from. If you apply an *__Ability__* to a target that is already referencing an **Ability** with its name, the old **Ability's Effects** are replaced with the new **Ability's Effects**, even if they're entirely different **Abilities**.

## Gear
Characters can use various pieces of *__Gear__* to further augment their capabilities. *__Gear__* comes in many forms, such as *Consumable* or *Equipment*.

A character may equip one *Head* *__Gear__*, one *Body* *__Gear__*, one *Foot* *__Gear__*, up to two *Hand* *__Gear__*, and up to 3 *Accessory* *__Gear__* at a given time, although this can change in various ways during gameplay. Each piece of *__Gear__* comes with a set of *Tags* that help explain how they functionally work and feel. *__Gear__* may also bestow new **Abilities** to characters that equip those pieces.

*__Gear__* also has **Density** and **Durability**; The more **Density** a piece of *__Gear__* has, the more durable and heavy it is. **Density** works similarly to a character's **<i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved"></i>Global Defense** in that it blocks all forms of incoming damage dealt to it. **Durability**, on the other hand, works like a character's **<a style="color:#ff4040"><i class="fa-solid fa-heart"></i>Health</a>**; If a piece of *__Gear's__* **<a style="color:#ff4040"><i class="fa-solid fa-heart"></i>Health</a>** ever hits zero, that piece of *__Gear__* is completely destroyed.

Additionally, *__Gear__* has **Heft** and **Weight**. A piece of *__Gear's__* **Weight** is also determined by its **Size**; Like a character of that particular **Size**, a piece of *__Gear's__* **Heft** related to that **Size**, and its **Weight** is equal to its **Density** x **Heft**.

## Defense
Characters, usually those wearing armor, have *__<i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved"></i>Defense__* as their secondary form of **Damage Mitigation**. *__<i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved"></i>Defense__* comes in many flavors, separated into two groups; *__<i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved"></i>Global Defense__* and *__<i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved"></i>Conditional Defense__*.

*__<i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved"></i>Global Defense__* is stronger, as it protects against *every* form damage can and may take within the game. However, it's rarely found compared to *__<i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved"></i>Conditional Defense__*. *__<i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved"></i>Conditional Defense__* is more frequently found, but is weaker as it has restrictions to what it applies to. Each *__<i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved"></i>Conditional Defense__* you have can stack within their specific parameters, as well as stacking with *__<i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved"></i>Global Defense__*.

Characters are initially created having 0 *__<i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved"></i>Global Defense__*.

## Evasion
Characters have *__<i class="fa-solid fa-person-falling"></i>Evasion__* as both the primary form of **Effect Mitigation** and **Damage Mitigation**. *__<i class="fa-solid fa-person-falling"></i>Evasion__* scales as though it's an **Aptitude** and is *strictly Global*; It takes effects whenever a character is defending against any **d% Check** and adds directly to that character's roll result as the roll's **Aptitude** value.

*__<i class="fa-solid fa-person-falling"></i>Evasion__* is optional; Since it functions as an **Aptitude**, you may have other **Aptitudes** to choose from in place of it. Even if you don't, you may choose not to use *__<i class="fa-solid fa-person-falling"></i>Evasion__* and instead set the **Aptitude** portion of the **d% Check** equal to -50 to make yourself more likely to hit.
