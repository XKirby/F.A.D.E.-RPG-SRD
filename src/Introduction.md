# Introduction
Welcome to the System Reference Document for the Tabletop Roleplaying Game of F.A.D.E., or "Fighter's Arena, Destructive Encounters". This page will give a rundown on the core concepts and philosophies found within the game.

## Core Concepts
The following *__Core Concepts__* of this TTRPG system are designed to introduce you to the basic rules found within the game:

### Base Stats
Similar to games made with the *Tri-Stat System*, such as *"Big Eyes, Small Mouth"*, this system utilizes only three *__Base Stats__*; **<a style="color:#ff4040">Physique</a>**, **<a style="color:#ffff40">Ego</a>**, and **<a style="color:#4040ff">Instinct</a>**.

**<a style="color:#ff4040">Physique</a>:** This *__Base Stat__* represents your physical presence and being. The higher it is, the heartier and more physically threatening you are.

**<b style="color:#ffff40">Ego</b>:** This *__Base Stat__* represents your mental strength and knowledge. The higher it is, the more inquisitive and mentally threatening you are.

**<a style="color:#4040ff">Instinct</a>:** This *__Base Stat__* represents your willpower and subconscious nature. The higher it is, the more naturally intelligent and strong you are.

### Aptitudes
Each character made with this system has a set of *__Aptitudes__* that tell you how effective they are with certain abilities or tasks. Each *__Aptitude__* has an assigned value from -50 to +50 which directly influences your **d% Checks**. If multiple *__Aptitudes__* are viable for a given **d% Check**, use the highest *__Aptitude__* value applicable to the situation.

### d% Checks
This system's **Checks** rely on rolls made with two 10-sided dice, or more commonly known as **d%**, to determine their overall outcome. Roll results made with *__d%__* range from 1 to 100. Rolling double zeroes results in the maximum of 100.

**Checks** have a *Target Value* and utilize the following formula for comparison: **Stat** + **Aptitude** + *__d%__*.

If the *Target Value* is less than or equal to the formula's result *or* you roll 96 or higher, the **Check** is a **Success**. Otherwise, if the *Target Value* is less than or equal to the formula's result *or* you roll 5 or lower, it's a **Failure**. Additionally, if a **Success** result overcomes the *Target Value* by 50 or more, the **Check** is a *__Critical Success__*. On the other hand, if the *Target Value* overcomes a **Failure** result by 50 or more, it's a *__Critical Failure__*. *__Critical Successes__* and *__Critical Failures__* change the severity of the result's outcome, for better or worse.

### Contested d% Checks
If two or more opposing characters within the game interact, the way to determine the outcome's results are through *__Contested d% Checks__*. For the most part, *__Contested d% Checks__* are performed using the same rules as normal, except that the defender's *Target Value* is *also* determined with **Stat** + **Aptitude** + **d%**. If the defender succeeds, the attacker fails, and vice versa.

### Abilities and Action Points
This system utilizes a pool of *__<i class="fa-solid fa-gem"></i>Actions Points__* you can use on your turn to perform *__Abilities__* while in a dangerous situation, such as combat or hazardous areas. When not in a dangerous situation, *__Abilities__* that cost *__Action Points__* take 2 seconds per *__<i class="fa-solid fa-gem"></i>Action Point__* spent to perform them. *__Abilities__* can cost anywhere from 0 to 3 *__<i class="fa-solid fa-gem"></i>Action Points__*, among other things dictated by their **Properties**.

Once an *__Ability__* activates, it enters a *Stack* of *__Abilities__*. Once *__Abilities__* have finished entering the *Stack*, they begin resolving, specifically in First-In-Last-Out order, or FILO for short; In other words, the last *__Ability__* in the *Stack* is the first to resolve. **Effects** from a single resolving *__Ability__* are interpreted in First-In-First-Out order, or FIFO for short; In other words, they resolve the way they're written, one **Effect** at a time. *__Abilities__* continue to resolve this way until the *Stack* is empty; while this is happening, no other *__Abilities__* may enter the current *Stack*.

### Effect Mitigation
Unlike other more noteworthy systems, this system has both *Defense* and *Evasion* as *__Effect Mitigation__*. **<i class="fa-solid fa-person-falling"></i>Evasion** functions as though it's an **Aptitude**, meaning it scales from -50 to +50, and is your primary *__Effect Mitigation__*. **<i class="fa-solid fa-shield-halved"></i>Defense** functions as a flat value with potential restrictions, with that value reducing incoming damage related to its restrictions to a minimum of 0, and is your secondary *__Effect Mitigation__*.

## Core Philosophies
The *__Core Philosophies__* of this TTRPG system will help you understand what to expect from its design and intent. They are as followed:
- <u>*Action Inspired:*</u> This will be mostly a combat-focused RPG, relying on action sequences inspired by comic books, fighting games, and action media tropes.
- <u>*Simplicity First:*</u> The main functionality of the game should be simple to understand at first glance. With this in mind, abilities and the effects they can produce should be short and to the point.
- <u>*Unleashed Potential:*</u> The game should feel impactful to play, allowing the players to reach heights of both power and creativity within your games.
- <u>*Clear Expectations:*</u> Whether playing the game or hosting a session, people have a shared goal in mind; To have fun with the game, cooperating in terms of storytelling and gameplay. With great power comes even greater responsibility; The expectations you and your players have should align closely enough to not cause problems that last outside of the game.
