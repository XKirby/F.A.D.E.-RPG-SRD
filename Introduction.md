# Introduction
Welcome to the System Reference Document for the Tabletop Roleplaying Game of F.A.D.E., or "Fighter's Arena, Destructive Encounters". This page will give a rundown on the core concepts and philosophies found within the game.

## Core Concepts
The following **Core Concepts** of this TTRPG system are designed to introduce you to the basic rules and mechanics found within the game:
- *__Three Stat System:__* Similar to games made with the Tri-Stat system, such as **"Small Eyes, Big Mouth"**, this system utilizes only three major Stats; **Body**, **Mind**, and **Soul**.
  - **Body** represents your physical presence and being.
  - **Mind** represents your mental strength and knowledge.
  - **Soul** represents your willpower and instinctual nature.
- *__Percentile Rolls:__* This system's Checks rely on rolls made with two 10-sided dice, or more commonly known as **d%** rolls, to determine their overall outcome. Rolls made with **d%** range from 1 to 100, with rolling double zeroes resulting in the maximum of 100.
  - Checks have a **Target Value** and utilize the following formula for comparison; **Stat** + **d%**. If the Check's **Target Value** is less than or equal to the formula's result, you succeed. Otherwise, you fail.
- *__Action Points:__* This system utilizes a pool of **Actions Points** you can use on your turn to perform actions while in a dangerous situation.
- *__Defense and Evasion:__* Unlike other more noteworthy systems, this system has both **Defense** and **Evasion**.
  - **Defense** reduces *all* damage you would receive directly by its value. For example, if you receive 10 damage and you have 6 **Defense**, you take only 4 damage.
  - **Evasion** increases your odds of outright dodging incoming effects directly by its value. For example, if an opponent's **d%** result is 100 and you have 60 **Evasion**, that result is dropped to 40.

## Core Philosophies
The **Core Philosophies** of this TTRPG system will help you understand what to expect from its design and intent. They are as followed:
- *__Action Inspired:__* This will be mostly a combat-focused RPG, relying on action sequences inspired by comic books, fighting games, and action media tropes.
- *__Simplicity First:__* The main functionality of the game should be simple to understand at first glance. With this in mind, abilities and the effects they can produce should be short and to the point.
- *__Unleashed Potential:__* The game should feel impactful to play, allowing the players to reach heights of both power and creativity within your games.
- *__Clear Expectations:__* Whether playing the game or hosting a session, people have a shared goal in mind; To have fun with the game, cooperating in terms of storytelling and gameplay. With great power comes even greater responsibility; The expectations you and your players have should align closely enough to not cause problems that last outside of the game.
  - As a *Player*, you are one of the main focus points of the game and have power over the game world and its inhabitants.
  - As a *Gamemaster*, you are the force that drives the story forward and have direct control over the interwoven nature of your world and its features, including the players' actions within it.
