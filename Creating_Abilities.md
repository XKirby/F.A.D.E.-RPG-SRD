# Creating Abilities
This page lists a large set of information related to *__Creating Abilities__*. When *__Creating Abilities__*, you'll want to conceptualize how the **Ability** will actively function. Once you have the **Ability** you want in mind, come back to this page to figure out how to piece it together.

> [!Warning]
> **Creating Abilities** is a time-consuming process; You'll be building **Abilities** *piece by piece* to get the desired effect. I recommend starting small and simple, building things up slowly over time as you **Level Up**. Once you get the hang of it, feel free to make your **Abilities** more complex and interesting.

## Ability Properties
As mentioned under the **Core Rules** page, **Abilities** have various *__Ability Properties__* that define basic information about how to utilize them. Most *__Ability Properties__* are used to restrict the **Ability** they're associated to to specific circumstances and use cases. They are listed and explained below:
- *Type:* The *Type* of an **Ability** is either *Active* or *Innate*; *Active* **Abilities** require you to specifically activate them before they can take effect, while *Innate* **Abilities** are always active within other *__Ability Property__* restrictions.
- *Prerequisites:* The **Ability** can't be learned by others unless they meet the *Prerequisites*. *Prerequisites* can be one or more of the following:
  - *Level:* The **Ability** can only be learned at this *Level* or higher.
  - *Aptitude:* The **Ability** can only be learned if you have this *Aptitude* at the listed value or higher.
  - *Natural Base Stats:* The **Ability** can only be learned if your *Base Stats* before *Gear* and **Ability Boosts** and **Penalties** are at the listed values or higher.
- *Costs:* The *Costs* of an **Ability** list what it consumes before it can be utilized. You can't spend what you don't have. *Costs* can be one or more of the following:
  - *Action Points:* The **Ability** requires you to spend this many *Action Points* before it can be utilized.
  - *Resources:* **Health**, **Stamina**, and/or **Energy** must be spent before the **Ability** can be utilized. You don't have the *Resources* to spend if you have less than the required amount above the minimum of 0 in that *Resource*.
  - *Gear:* The **Ability** must consume specific pieces of *Gear* before it can be utilized.
